# Hearing mentions in VA-led trials of the matched strata, read

Read on 2026-10-15 from the ClinicalTrials.gov API v2. CC BY 4.0.
Made by MV2 of Munim, Inc. with AI agents.

[VA-MATCHED-RESULT.md](VA-MATCHED-RESULT.md) found that, within the same conditions, VA-led
no-phase trials name a hearing wording 1.77 times as often as other trials. These are
mentions. This note reads them.

**Which trials.** The first 20 of the 30 VA-led trials in the five matched strata (PTSD,
substance use, depression, pain, cardiometabolic; no phase; trials of cognitive, brain-injury,
stroke, hearing and eye conditions left out) whose criteria name the hearing family, in the order
the API returned them. This is not a random sample. Each item was quoted from the record by the
API reader. Three were checked again against the single record (NCT07202325, NCT01001546,
NCT05681520) and matched word for word.

**All 20 shut out people with hearing loss:** 19 in the exclusion criteria and 1 as an inclusion

## The pattern: telephone and video

- **6 of the 20 items tie the exclusion to a telephone or computer** in the item itself (phone
  calls, telephone interviews or assessments, phone access, "a computer or telephone"), and one
  more to watching video. One more study is telephone-based by its title. Many of these are
  smoking-cessation, pain and mental-health programmes delivered by phone.
- **Several are broad.** Eight items are a bare phrase ("Hearing impairment", "Difficulty
  hearing", "Severely impaired hearing") with no threshold and no link to a study task.
- **Where the reason is the telephone, there is an accommodation to offer instead of an
  exclusion:** captioned telephone, relay services (711 in the US), text-based contact, or video
  calls with captions or a sign language interpreter. The hearing section of the guide
  ([toolkit/inclusive-eligibility-guide.md](../../toolkit/inclusive-eligibility-guide.md))
  suggests "text or relay services instead of phone-only contact".

## Limits

- The first 20 the API returned, not a random sample, from 30 VA-led trials. The other 10 were not
  read.
- The registry search expands synonyms, so 8 items matched through wordings such as "impaired
  hearing" or "difficulty hearing".
- Registry criteria are often a summary of the protocol. A protocol may offer an accommodation
  the registry text does not mention.
- This does not say any of these exclusions is unjustified for its study. It says what the
  criteria say, and where an accommodation could be considered.
