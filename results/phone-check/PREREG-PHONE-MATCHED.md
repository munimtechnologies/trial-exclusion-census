# Pre-registration: do telephone-delivered trials name hearing loss more often?

Written 2026-10-15, before any phrase was counted for this test. Only the twelve stratum sizes
below were counted. Do not edit this file; additions go in a new file and are labelled
exploratory. [prereg-phone-matched.json](prereg-phone-matched.json) holds the same design in a
form `phone_matched.py` 0.1.0 is tested against. CC BY 4.0.
Made by MV2 of Munim, Inc. with AI agents.

## Why

[VA-HEARING-READ.md](../sponsor-check/VA-HEARING-READ.md) read 20 hearing mentions in VA-led
trials. All 20 shut people with hearing loss out, and at least 6 tie it to telephone or computer
contact ("Hearing impairment that precludes completing telephone assessments"). Single protocols
outside the VA do the same: NCT03609658 excludes "moderate to severe hearing loss (due to phone
interventions)". A web search on 2026-10-15 found no registry-wide study of this.

The question: **across all sponsors, do trials delivered by telephone name a hearing wording in
their eligibility criteria more often than other trials of the same condition, and more than
they name phrases that have nothing to do with hearing?** If so, the telephone is a general cause
of hearing exclusions with known fixes (captioned telephone, relay services such as 711, text,
video with captions), not a VA habit.

## Design

- **Trials:** interventional, no phase (`AREA[Phase]NA`), all start years. Trials whose own
  condition is cognitive, brain injury, stroke, hearing or vision are left out of every stratum
  (`va_matched.OWN`).
- **Exposure:** `AREA[InterventionSearch]telephone`. The registry's search treats telephone and
  phone as one word: `telephone`, `telephone OR phone` and `telephone OR "phone call" OR "phone
  calls" OR "by phone"` each gave 8,657 no-phase trials on 2026-10-15. So the exposure also takes
  in phone follow-up and mobile-phone apps, which should pull the ratio toward 1.
- **Comparator:** every other trial of the same stratum (stratum count minus telephone count).
- **Strata,** disjoint, in priority order: the five of `va_matched.py` (PTSD; substance use;
  depression; pain; cardiometabolic), then **other**: every trial that matches none of the five.
- **Phrases:** the twelve of `va_matched.py`: the cognitive, hearing and visual families; 8
  controls (pregnant, hepatitis, allergy, migraine, osteoporosis, claustrophobia, celiac,
  hernia), each counted without the trials of its own condition; and "informed consent" as a
  reference.
- **Measure:** the Mantel-Haenszel ratio of shares, telephone over other, across the six strata,
  with the Greenland-Robins 95% interval. Unmatched and per-stratum ratios are reported beside
  it. A control with fewer than 5 telephone matches expected is left out of HP2 and S1.
- **Counts:** 12 stratum sizes and 144 phrase counts, 156 in all, each the `totalCount` of the
  query `phone_matched.term()` builds.

## Hypotheses

- **HP1 (primary):** the hearing family's matched 95% interval lies above 1.
- **HP2 (primary, specificity):** the hearing family's matched ratio is larger than the visual
  family's and larger than every kept control's. Talking on the telephone needs hearing, not
  sight. If telephone trials simply had fuller criteria, visual and the controls would rise too.
  If nothing set hearing apart, it would come first by chance 1 time in (rivals + 1), for example
  1 in 10 with all 8 controls kept. This is a ranking, not a p-value, and is reported as such.
- **How the results will be read:** HP1 and HP2 both hold: telephone delivery goes with more
  hearing exclusions across sponsors, beyond a general fullness of criteria. HP1 holds and HP2
  does not: telephone trials name many things more often and hearing is not singled out. HP1
  fails: no evidence of a general link.
- **S1 (secondary):** the rank test of `va_matched.py`: the three families against the kept
  controls (exact one-sided rank sum).
- **S2 (secondary, reading):** read the first 20 trials, in the API's default order, whose
  criteria name a hearing wording, among telephone trials **not** led by the VA:

  `AREA[StudyType]INTERVENTIONAL AND AREA[Phase]NA AND AREA[InterventionSearch]telephone AND AREA[EligibilityCriteria](<hearing family>) AND NOT AREA[ConditionSearch](<OWN>) AND NOT AREA[LeadSponsorName](VA OR Veteran OR Veterans)`

  and the first 20 of the same query with `NOT AREA[InterventionSearch]telephone` in place of
  the exposure. For each trial, quote the item that names hearing and mark it:
  - **phone-tied:** the item itself names telephone, phone, calls, or interviews or assessments
    by phone;
  - **shuts out:** an exclusion, or an inclusion requirement of hearing;
  - **not shutting out:** anything else (an accommodation, a hearing-loss group that is included,
    a wording that only matched by synonym).

  Predictions: at least 5 of the 20 telephone items are phone-tied; at most 2 of the 20 others
  are. The two phone-tied counts are compared with a one-sided Fisher exact test.
- Not tested: whether any exclusion is justified for its study.

## Limits known in advance

- Mentions anywhere in the criteria, not hand-checked exclusions (S2 reads 40 of them).
- The exposure is a search on the intervention text and takes in phone apps and phone
  follow-up; a trial delivered by telephone that never says so is counted as other.
- Matching is on condition only, not on age, sponsor, design or start year. The "other" stratum
  is large and mixed.
- The registry search expands synonyms, and counts change as records are added. All counts will be
  made on one day.
